NHTSA Recall Number: 23V-678
Manufacturer: Nissan North America, Inc.
Model: Altima
Year: 2023
Component: Headlight Assembly - LED Driver Module
Manufacture Dates: May 2023 - September 2023
Affected Vehicles: Approximately 74,000

Description:
The LED headlight driver module may fail due to a defective voltage regulator circuit. When the module fails, one or both headlights may suddenly stop working, significantly reducing visibility for the driver and making the vehicle less visible to other drivers. This increases the risk of a crash, particularly during nighttime or adverse weather conditions.

The failure occurs when the voltage regulator overheats under continuous operation. High ambient temperatures or prolonged headlight use (such as during extended highway driving at night) accelerate the failure. The headlight may fail completely and not illuminate at all, or it may flicker intermittently before total failure.

Warning signs include:
- Headlight flickering
- Dim or reduced light output from one or both headlights
- Headlight warning indicator on instrument panel
- One headlight significantly dimmer than the other
- Complete loss of one or both low-beam headlights

The high-beam function may continue to work even when low-beams fail, providing temporary visibility until repair. Daytime running lights and turn signals are not affected by this issue.

This recall affects 2023 Nissan Altima sedan models including S, SV, SR, SL, and Platinum trim levels equipped with LED headlights. Halogen headlight models are not affected.

Remedy:
Nissan dealers will inspect both headlight assemblies and replace the LED driver modules with an improved design that includes enhanced thermal management and updated voltage regulator circuitry.

The repair procedure includes:
- Diagnostic scan for headlight system fault codes
- Inspection of both headlight assemblies
- Replacement of LED driver modules in both headlights
- Software update to headlight control system (version H23-001 or later)
- Functional verification of all lighting functions
- Light aim adjustment and verification

Even if only one headlight has failed, both LED driver modules will be replaced to prevent future failure of the other side.

Repair time is approximately 1.5-2 hours. All parts, labor, and software updates are provided free of charge.

Owner Action:
If you experience headlight failure or flickering:

IMMEDIATE actions if headlights fail while driving:
1. Turn on high beams if available (may still function)
2. Turn on hazard flashers to increase visibility
3. Reduce speed and drive cautiously
4. Use fog lights if equipped
5. Pull over safely if visibility is insufficient
6. Schedule immediate dealer service

For proactive recall service:
- Schedule service at your convenience
- Inspection can be performed during regular maintenance
- Both modules will be replaced even if symptoms only on one side

Temporary Lighting Solutions:
If headlights fail and immediate service is not available:
- Use high beams where legal and safe (switch to low beam for oncoming traffic)
- Use fog lights if equipped
- Avoid driving at night or in poor visibility conditions
- Schedule service as soon as possible
- Do not delay - driving without proper headlights is dangerous and illegal

Driving Restrictions:
Most states require functioning headlights for nighttime driving. Until repair:
- Limit driving to daylight hours if possible
- Avoid night driving unless absolutely necessary
- If you must drive at night, use alternative lighting (high beams/fog lights)
- Be aware of local laws regarding headlight requirements

Parts Availability:
Nissan has prioritized production of replacement LED driver modules. If parts are temporarily unavailable:
- Dealer will place urgent parts order
- You'll be notified immediately when parts arrive
- Nissan may provide rental vehicle for customers unable to drive safely
- High-beam function can be used as interim solution where legal

Preventive Maintenance:
To help identify potential headlight issues early:
- Check headlight operation regularly
- Have someone observe both headlights while you cycle them on/off
- Check for even brightness between left and right sides
- Monitor for flickering or dimming
- Report any abnormalities to your dealer during routine service

Additional Lighting Inspection:
When you bring your vehicle for recall service, ask the dealer to inspect:
- All exterior lighting functions
- Light aim and adjustment
- Lens condition (cracks, moisture, yellowing)
- Wiring harness connections
- Battery and charging system (weak battery can stress lighting modules)
