NHTSA Recall Number: 22V-445
Manufacturer: General Motors LLC
Model: Equinox
Year: 2022
Component: Fuel Tank Strap
Manufacture Dates: September 2021 - March 2022
Affected Vehicles: Approximately 112,000

Description:
The fuel tank mounting straps may corrode prematurely, weakening the structural integrity of the straps. In severe cases, a strap can break, allowing the fuel tank to drop from the vehicle while driving. A detached or partially detached fuel tank creates multiple serious safety hazards including fuel leaks, fire risk, road hazards for other vehicles, and loss of vehicle control.

The issue is caused by inadequate corrosion protection coating on the steel mounting straps. When exposed to road salt, moisture, and environmental contaminants, the straps corrode much faster than designed. Vehicles operated in northern climates with road salt exposure or coastal areas with salt air are at higher risk.

The corrosion typically begins at stress points where the straps contact the fuel tank or frame mounting brackets. Visual inspection may show rust, pitting, or thinning of the strap material. In advanced stages, the strap may show visible cracks or separation.

⚠️ WARNING: A fuel leak or detached fuel tank presents serious fire and crash risks. If you notice fuel odor, see fuel dripping under your vehicle, or hear unusual noises from the fuel tank area, stop driving immediately and have the vehicle towed to a dealer.

This recall affects 2022 Chevrolet Equinox models including L, LS, LT, Premier, and RS trim levels with both the 1.5L and 2.0L turbocharged engines.

Remedy:
GM dealers will inspect the fuel tank mounting straps and replace them with improved corrosion-resistant straps manufactured with enhanced protective coating. The repair procedure includes:

1. Vehicle lift and undercarriage inspection
2. Fuel tank support with safety jack
3. Removal of both fuel tank mounting straps
4. Inspection of fuel tank for damage
5. Cleaning of mounting surfaces
6. Installation of new corrosion-resistant straps
7. Application of additional corrosion protection
8. Torque verification (40 Nm / 30 lb-ft)
9. Fuel system pressure test
10. Road test for proper fuel tank security

The repair takes approximately 2-3 hours. All parts and labor are free.

If significant corrosion damage is found on other components (fuel tank, lines, or mounting brackets), those will also be replaced at no charge.

Owner Action:
IMMEDIATE inspection recommended if you notice:
- Strong fuel odor near vehicle
- Fuel visible under vehicle
- Wet spots under vehicle after parking
- Unusual scraping or dragging noises
- Visible corrosion or rust on fuel tank straps (if visible during tire changes, etc.)

For vehicles in high-corrosion areas (states that use road salt):
- Schedule inspection as soon as possible
- Dealers will prioritize these vehicles

For all affected vehicles:
- Schedule inspection at convenience
- Visual inspection can be performed during regular service visits

High-Risk Areas:
This recall is especially critical for vehicles operated in:
- Northern states with winter road salt (salt belt states)
- Coastal areas with salt air exposure
- Areas with high humidity
- Regions with poor road conditions

Interim Safety Measures:
Until recall repair is completed:
1. Inspect under vehicle weekly for fuel leaks
2. Park outdoors away from buildings if fuel odor present
3. Avoid filling fuel tank completely (maintain ½ to ¾ full)
4. Monitor for unusual noises from fuel tank area
5. Have vehicle towed if fuel leak suspected - do not drive

Refueling Safety:
Until repair completed:
- Fill fuel tank slowly to avoid splashing
- Do not overfill or "top off" tank
- Check for leaks after refueling
- Wipe up any spilled fuel immediately

- Fuel tank cannot be safely driven to dealer

Extended Inspection:
For vehicles showing strap corrosion, GM recommends:
- Annual undercarriage inspection
- Fuel system inspection at each oil change
- Immediate inspection after driving through heavy road salt
- Undercarriage washing after winter season

Additional Protection:
After recall repair, consider:
- Regular undercarriage washing in winter months
- Application of undercoating (dealer can recommend products)
- Inspection during routine maintenance

If Fuel Tank Becomes Detached:
If you hear a loud dragging noise and suspect the fuel tank has dropped:
1. Immediately turn on hazard lights
2. Safely pull over to shoulder
3. Turn off engine
4. Exit vehicle and move away
5. Call emergency services (911) if fuel leak present
6. Call GM Roadside Assistance for towing: 1-800-243-8872
